\subsubsection{Author review and post hoc answerability sensitivity}
The author review classified 15 references as supported under ordinary table-question conventions, six as interpretation dependent and three as priority disputes (Table~\ref{tab:reference-review}). These classifications concern answerability rather than proven label errors. Omitting the three priority disputes left 21 paired tasks. The interactions were 0.0 points [0.0, 0.0] for Qwen-Plus, $-33.3$ [$-61.9$, 0.0] for Qwen3.5-Plus and $-47.6$ [$-66.7$, $-28.6$] for DeepSeek-V3. Correction-condition correct counts were unchanged at each endpoint, while the denominator fell from 24 to 21.

A further post hoc check retained only the 15 category A tasks. The interactions were 0.0 points [0.0, 0.0], $-46.7$ [$-80.0$, $-6.7$] and $-53.3$ [$-80.0$, $-26.7$], respectively (Table~\ref{tab:reference-sensitivity}). These supported-task results retained the directions of the full-sample estimates. Category A denotes support under table-question conventions, not independent adjudication or guaranteed absence of ambiguity. Both subset analyses were conducted after the original results were known. All 24-task results remain primary.
